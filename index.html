The Artist

Meet Cassy MacDonald

Hidden away in the wooded hills of New Hampshire, Cassy MacDonald makes things from trees. Not just things — presences. Objects that seem to remember what the forest felt like.

Her work spans pyrography, relief carving, lathe-turning, resin casting, and hand-painting — sometimes all in the same piece. There's always a little wildness in it.

Why Wary?

The Bird. The Film. The Name.

"The Cassowary doesn't explain itself.
It simply exists — ancient, armored, and utterly itself."

The name Cassy is Wary walks two paths at once. The Cassowary — that magnificent, terrifying, sapphire-throated bird — is the shop's spirit animal. Ancient and unafraid. A little dangerous.

The other half nods to Beau is Afraid — that surreal, dreamlike film about dread and wonder existing in the same breath. That feeling lives in this work too. You find something beautiful here, and it looks back at you.

Black and blue. Forest and feather. Cozy and uncanny. That's the vibe. That's Cassy is Wary.
